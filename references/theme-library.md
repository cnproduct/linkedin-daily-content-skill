# Theme diversity library

Choose one family × one angle × one audience; inspect recent captions for semantic duplicates. Do not randomly recycle yesterday's structure with a new title. Random choice is acceptable only among eligible, relevant options. These are concept directions, not claims about inventory or manufacturability.

## Buyer angles

- Structure comparison: four alternative solutions to one use need.
- Sample review: four visible details to examine (fit, edges, opening access, component layout).
- Development brief: dimensions, opening mechanism, colors, packaging requirements.
- Use scenario: commute, desk break, picnic, travel; avoid unsupported child safety claims.
- Channel comparison: retail shelf, gift program, specialty store, distributor assortment.
- Packaging idea: space planning, component arrangement, unpacking sequence, visual identity.
- Assortment strategy: related sizes, accessories, replacement-part questions, display grouping.
- Buyer Q&A: what information is needed to assess MOQ, prototypes, timing or compliance; give no invented numbers.
- Design tradeoff: simplicity vs removable parts, compactness vs capacity, access vs closure complexity; present questions, not tested outcomes.
- Behind the scenes: actual employees checking relevant real samples, packing, or discussing orders.
- Document review: only actual matching readable documents; discuss what is shown without extending the conclusion.
- Product detail spotlight: actual visible detail, no unsupported performance claim.

## Four-card narratives

A. Hero → component detail → scenario → buyer question.
B. Direction 1 → direction 2 → direction 3 → direction 4, all addressing the same development brief.
C. Buyer need → review points → packaging/display considerations → next-step checklist.
D. Four authentic moments from one real sample/packing workflow.
E. Actual product overview → supported visible structure → actual packaging → requested sourcing information.

## Visual variation

Rotate studio hero, close-up, top-down layout, exploded **concept** view, lifestyle **concept** scene, genuine workplace photograph, and deterministic photo-with-caption layout. Use a coherent palette within each set. Do not add fictional staff, customer endorsements, factory scenes, certificates, or logos to create variety.

## Selection record

Keep a local record with date, semantic theme, product family, buyer angle, source files/type, and final filenames. Avoid identical themes for 14 days, recent structures when alternatives exist, and consecutive generic categories. If a user requests repetition or a specific theme, follow that choice and record it.
